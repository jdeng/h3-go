What this model can do

FL2VA generates video with stereo sound from text, a first frame, a last frame, or both. No ComfyUI or Python inference process is involved.
Model, limits & useful defaults
  • Diffusion: pruned INT8 ConvRot FL2VA; text encoder: H3 Qwen3-VL-32B Q4_K_M; video VAE: FP16; audio VAE: FP32.
  • 24 fps, 32-kHz stereo. Frame counts snap upward to 17k+5. A 10-second request produces 243 frames / 10.125 seconds, not exactly 10 seconds.
  • Dimensions round to 32 pixels. Custom dimensions allow exact ratios such as 576×384 for 3:2. Keyframes are resized to the output canvas; matching their ratio avoids stretching.
  • Turbo is faster, with potential motion/audio tradeoffs. Turn it off for base-model sampling. Sub-4-second clips are useful tests, not the official 4–15-second target range.
  • Disabling audio skips audio VAE decoding and MP4 audio; joint audio/video diffusion still runs.
  • Multiple subject references, reference audio/video, editing masks and 2K regeneration are not enabled by this local FL2VA setup.
